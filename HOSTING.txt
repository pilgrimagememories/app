DELMONT PILGRIMAGE - OFFLINE WEB APP
Upload ALL files in this folder to one folder on an https:// site
(for example app.delmontpilgrimage.com or delmontpilgrimage.com/app/):
  index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png
Test: open the address on a phone, wait for it to load once, then use
"Install app" / "Add to Home screen". Switch to airplane mode and open it.
Updating: change index.html, then change V='dcp-v1' in sw.js to 'dcp-v2' so phones refresh.
