Your Pilgrimage

It starts at the Shields Farm Historic Log Cabin. Step outside and walk the field: about 30 minutes, eight live-action scenes.

  • Mary and Joseph meet the angelsThe story begins.
  • The journey to BethlehemWalk the road with them.
  • Shepherds and wise menHear the Good News told.
  • Rejoice in Christ’s birthLive animals, dramatic music and soft lighting along the way.

Don’t miss the donkey

Visit the interactive Mary and Joseph tent to pet and take your picture with a live donkey.

Shuttle and churches

Five Delmont churches offer activities and food. Free shuttle buses run between them and Shield’s Farm, before or after your walk.

Pilgrimage Junior families: park at a participating church and board the shuttle at least 30 minutes before your ticketed start time.
